# RevPilot AI product requirements

Revenue intelligence and approval-controlled sales operations for B2B teams.
The complete acceptance target combines a PostgreSQL 18 data platform, governed
SQL/dbt analytics, four predictive services, tool-grounded investigations, durable
Temporal automation and nine responsive Next.js workspaces.

The demonstration organisation is Northstar Cloud: reproducible synthetic
commercial history, clearly labelled throughout. CSV/Excel, HubSpot and Stripe
are additional ingestion modes. External credentials are optional; missing
configuration must never be replaced with fake responses or success states.

Nine workspaces: overview, pipeline, forecasting, customers, analyst,
automations, recovery, reports and operations. Interface implementation must
follow the design specification and be reviewed using actual screenshots at
1440px, 768px and 390px. Every control must persist or query through an actual
backend, with loading, empty, error and stale states.

Final acceptance requires tenant isolation, OIDC sessions, financial consistency,
point-in-time ML evaluation, audited tools, exact-payload approval, idempotent
execution, real exports, tested connectors, reproducible startup, CI and verified
hosting where authorised. Separate accounting recognition from invoicing,
bookings and cash. Do not claim completed features or deployments without evidence.

See implementation-status.md for current coverage and unfinished acceptance work.
